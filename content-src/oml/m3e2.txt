# Meet 3, Event 2: GEOMETRY — Right Triangles

@ Nov 2025
A: A ladder is leaning against a vertical wall of a tall building, forming a $60^\circ$ angle with the level ground. The base of the ladder is $6$ feet away from the wall. There is a spot of paint halfway up on the ladder. How high above the ground (in feet) is the spot of paint?
= 3\sqrt{3}
B: A right triangle has legs of length $x$ and $2.4x$. If the length of each side of the triangle is an integer, find the smallest possible length of the hypotenuse of this triangle.
= 13
C: In quadrilateral $MATH$, $\overline{MA}\perp\overline{MH}$ and $\overline{AH}\perp\overline{HT}$. If $MA=2$, $m\angle MAH=30^\circ$, and $m\angle HTA=45^\circ$, find $AT$.
= \frac{4\sqrt{6}}{3}
