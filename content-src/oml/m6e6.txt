@ Mar 2026
A: The first, second, and last terms of an arithmetic sequence are $b$, $2b$, and $4b$, respectively. If the sum of the first two terms is $15$, find the sum of the entire arithmetic sequence.
= 50
B: If $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{500}{x^n}=250$, find $x$.
= 3
C: The first $2$ terms of an arithmetic sequence are $a_1=\log_4 3$ and $a_2=\log_4 6$. Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{20}a_n$.
= 95+20\log_4 3

@ Mar 2022
A: Find $x$ so that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}9x^{n-1}=1$.
= \frac{1}{10}
B: In an arithmetic sequence the 5th term is $12$ and the 8th term is $-3$. Find the sum of the first $20$ terms of this sequence.
= -310
C: Let $S$ be the sum of the convergent geometric series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_1r^{n-1}$, $a_1\ne0$. Let $S_1$ be the sum obtained by adding up every other term of the series for $S$ beginning with the first term, and $S_2$ be the sum obtained by adding up every other term of the series for $S$ beginning with the second term. If $S_1-S_2=2S$, find the value of $\left(\dfrac{S_2}{S}\right)^3$.
= -\frac{1}{8}
