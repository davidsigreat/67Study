@ Apr 2025
A: Evaluate: $$\int_0^{5\pi/6}e^{\sin x}\cos x\,dx$$
= \sqrt{e}-1
B: Solve the differential equation $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+9}}$ with initial condition $y(4)=2$.
= y=\sqrt{x^2+9}-3
C: A region $R$ is bounded by the lines $x=1$ and $x=2$ and the curves $y=x^2-1$ and $y=\sqrt{x}-1$. Find the area of $R$.
= \frac{9-4\sqrt{2}}{3}

@ Apr 2023
A: Solve for $b$: $$3\int_1^2 x^2\,dx=\int_3^b\frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\,dx$$
= 52+14\sqrt{3}
B: Evaluate: $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac8n\sqrt{9+\frac{16}{n}k}$$
= \frac{98}{3}

= 20

@ Apr 2021
A: If $\displaystyle\int_1^2 x\sqrt{x-1}\,dx=\int_a^b\sqrt{u}\,(u+1)\,du$, find $a+b$.
= 1
B: Region $R$ is bound by the curves $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=x^2$ and is the base of a solid. Each cross section of this solid taken perpendicular to the $y$-axis is a rectangle whose height is three times the length of its base in region $R$. Find the volume of the solid.
= \frac{27}{70}
C: The temperature in Whoville $t$ hours after 9 a.m. is modeled by the function $F(t)=50+14\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi t}{12}\right)$. Find the average temperature during the period from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m.
= 50+\frac{28}{\pi}
