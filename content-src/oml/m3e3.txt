# Meet 3, Event 3: ALGEBRA I — Age, Motion, & Number Problems

@ Nov 2025
A: Twice Katie's age five years ago is equal to her age in seven years. How old is Katie now?
= 17
B: The speed of Zach's boat in still water is $15$ miles per hour. He is traveling upstream from Port Allen to Port Zante on a river with current $3$ miles per hour. The trip upstream takes $2$ hours longer than the trip downstream. What is the distance (in miles) between Port Allen and Port Zante?
= 72
C: Consider three consecutive integers between $0$ and $9$, inclusive. The largest two-digit number that can be formed by two of those integers is six more than the sum of the smallest two-digit number that can be formed by two of those integers and twice the average of the three integers. Find the sum of the three integers.
= 21

@ Nov 2024
A: Ms. Matsubara is $4$ years younger than Ms. Dao, and $20$ years from today, their ages will total $100$ years. Ms. Matsubara is in constant denial of her true age, and she claims that she is only $21$ years old every year on and after her $21$st birthday. Assuming that she has already celebrated her birthday this year, on how many birthdays has Ms. Matsubara claimed to be $21$ years old?
= 8
B: Taylor wrote down three consecutive odd integers from least to greatest for her classmates to find. She gave one hint. She said the sum of the first and last is three greater than the product of the first and second. Michael asked her if the three numbers are $1$, $3$, and $5$, but Taylor said that while those numbers work, those are not what she wrote down. What three consecutive odd integers did Taylor write down?
= -1, 1, 3
C: Kainalu left Mr. Yadao's classroom at 3:50 p.m. to walk home, leaving behind his cell phone. Mr. Yadao notices the cell phone at 4:10 p.m. and runs outside with the phone to catch Kainalu. Kainalu walks at $2$ miles per hour and Mr. Yadao, who is in a hurry, walks at $4$ miles per hour. At what time (p.m.) will Mr. Yadao catch up to Kainalu?
= 4:30
~ 4:30\text{ p.m.} ;; 4:30pm

@ Nov 2022

= 2069
B: The sum of three integers is $456$. The second number is double the first number. The third number is three times the sum of the first two numbers. Find the third number.
= 342
C: Bree traveling in her motor boat at a constant speed with the current can cover the same distance in the same amount of time as Cody traveling in his motor boat at a constant speed against the current. If Bree and Cody switch directions and travel at the same constant speeds as before, Bree will take five times as long to cover the same distance as Cody. If the current flows at a constant speed of $5$ miles per hour and in the same direction during all scenarios, what are the speeds (mph) of Bree's and Cody's motor boats in still water? (Answer: Bree, Cody)
= 10, 20
! Official answer: Bree: 10 mph; Cody: 20 mph.

@ Nov 2021

= 18
B: At midnight, Dane, Shane, and Lorraine start traveling west along Main Way. Dane is $100$ miles from Shane, and Shane is $300$ miles from Lorraine. Each of them travels at a constant speed. In six hours, Dane overtakes Shane, and in another six hours, Dane overtakes Lorraine. At what time will Shane overtake Lorraine? Indicate A.M. or P.M.
= 6:00\text{ P.M.}

If her total travel time is $2$ hours, what is Carolyn's speed (mph) with her jetpack when there is no wind and she is not carrying any supplies?
= 20

He then immediately turned around and ran back downhill along the same route, arriving at his starting point $12$ minutes later. His average speed for the entire run was $7.5$ miles per hour. If John's speed on level ground is the average of his uphill and downhill speeds, how many miles would John run on level ground in $45$ minutes?
