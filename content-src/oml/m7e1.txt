@ Apr 2026
A: A weighted four-sided die has faces numbered $1$, $2$, $3$, and $4$. The die is twice as likely to land on $4$ as on $2$, and twice as likely to land on $2$ as on $1$. Landing on $3$ is equally likely as landing on $1$. If the die is rolled once, what is the probability it lands on $3$?
= \frac{1}{8}
B: A bag contains $4$ red marbles, $6$ blue marbles, and $2$ green marbles. If three marbles are randomly drawn from the bag without replacement, what is the probability that at least one of the drawn marbles is green?
= \frac{5}{11}

The last blue pair has monkeys. Nick pulls two socks at random. If both socks are the same color, what is the probability that they also have matching animal patterns?
= \frac{7}{27}
